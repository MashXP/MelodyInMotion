How to Play

Step 1: Setup Octave
Show 1 to 5 extended fingers with your non-playing hand (default left) to set the octave. If you show 0 fingers, the sound mutes.

Step 2: Sign the Note
Use your playing hand (default right) to make the Solfège sign corresponding to the active note shown on the sheet music. Watch the yellow ghost guideline on camera if you're stuck!

Step 3: Play and Score
Match the correct note to play the sound and earn 1 point! To play consecutive identical notes, briefly release your hand gesture or change notes between play triggers.
